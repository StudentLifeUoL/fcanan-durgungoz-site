Public Engagement

Alongside my research, I have led Mersin University Faculty of Education student volunteer efforts to rebuild school spaces in earthquake-affected regions of Türkiye — including Hatay (Samandağ), Gaziantep (İslahiye), and Kahramanmaraş (Andırın) — through the Faculty's community service programme. Each space is dedicated to those lost in the 2023 earthquakes.

In total, I have helped build 3 school libraries, 3 Mind and Intelligence Games classrooms (Akıl ve Zeka Oyunları Sınıfı), and 3 special education classrooms across these earthquake-affected cities.

In recognition of this work, I received Mersin University's 2026 Social Contribution Award (Toplumsal Katkı Ödülü).

I also contributed to "Champion Angels" (Şampiyon Melekler), a memorial project led by Faculty of Education colleague Prof. Dr. Yusuf İnandı and developed through the Faculty's Extracurricular Activities in Education course. The project honours a youth volleyball team from Gazimağusa Turkish Maarif College — 35 athletes, coaches, and family members who died when the Grand İsias Hotel collapsed during the 6 February 2023 earthquake. In May 2025, we built a volleyball court at a secondary school and a Mind and Logic Games classroom at an elementary school in Çelikhan, Adıyaman, marking the opening with a memorial volleyball match between the affected school's team and Mersin University students.
