Public Engagement

Alongside her research, Dr. Durgungöz has led Mersin University Faculty of Education student volunteer efforts to build school libraries in earthquake-affected regions of Türkiye — including Hatay (Samandağ), Gaziantep (İslahiye), and Kahramanmaraş (Andırın) — through the Faculty's community service programme, with each library dedicated to those lost in the 2023 earthquakes.

3 school libraries rebuilt 2026 Social Contribution Award

In recognition of this work, she received Mersin University's 2026 Social Contribution Award (Toplumsal Katkı Ödülü).
