Profile

Understanding How Children Learn, Feel, and Recover

Her research follows children with developmental language disorder as they learn to read emotion — and the communities she works with as they rebuild after disaster.

Dr. Fatma Canan Durgungöz is an Honorary Research Fellow in the Department of Psychology at the University of Bath, following a postdoctoral fellowship there. She holds a PhD in Special and Inclusive Education from the University of Nottingham.

Her research centres on developmental language disorder (DLD), special educational needs and disabilities (SEND), and the thoughtful use of digital and AI-based tools in special education. Recent work has explored emotion-recognition interventions for children with DLD and their parents, technology-mediated participation for neurodivergent students in higher education, and AI-assisted tools for special education teaching.
