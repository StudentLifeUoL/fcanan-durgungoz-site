About

Researching how children with developmental language disorder understand emotion, how technology can make learning more inclusive, and how universities can support communities recovering from disaster.

Dr. Fatma Canan Durgungöz is an Assistant Professor (Dr. Öğr. Üyesi) in the Department of Special Education, Faculty of Education, at Mersin University, and an Honorary Research Fellow in the Department of Psychology at the University of Bath, following a postdoctoral fellowship there. She holds a PhD in Special and Inclusive Education from the University of Nottingham.

Her research centres on developmental language disorder (DLD), special educational needs and disabilities (SEND), and the thoughtful use of digital and AI-based tools in special education. Recent work has explored emotion-recognition interventions for children with DLD and their parents, technology-mediated participation for neurodivergent students in higher education, and AI-assisted tools for special education teaching.
