Profile

Fatma Canan Durgungöz

I am primarily interested in developmental language delay — how children experience it, and how technology, families, and educators can help them thrive.

I am an Honorary Research Fellow in the Department of Psychology at the University of Bath, following a postdoctoral fellowship there. I hold a PhD in Special and Inclusive Education from the University of Nottingham.

My research centres on developmental language disorder (DLD), special educational needs and disabilities (SEND), and the thoughtful use of digital and AI-based tools in special education. Recently I've explored emotion-recognition interventions for children with DLD and their parents, technology-mediated participation for neurodivergent students in higher education, and AI-assisted tools for special education teaching.
